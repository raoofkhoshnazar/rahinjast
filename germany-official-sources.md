## 1. Core Immigration Authorities

### BAMF — Federal Office for Migration and Refugees
- **URL:** https://www.bamf.de/EN
- **German:** Bundesamt für Migration und Flüchtlinge
- **Description:** The central federal authority responsible for all matters related to asylum, refugee protection, integration, and immigration to Germany. BAMF issues binding guidelines on residence permits, runs integration courses, and publishes official migration statistics. It is the primary regulatory body under the German Residence Act (AufenthG).
- **Key resources:**
  - Residence permit categories and eligibility
  - Asylum procedures and protection status
  - Integration course registration and providers
  - Annual migration reports and statistics
  - Recognition of foreign professional qualifications (in cooperation with BIBB/KMK)

---

### Make it in Germany — Official Skilled Worker Portal
- **URL:** https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en
- **Operated by:** Federal Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs (BMAS) + BAMF + Federal Employment Agency
- **Description:** The official government portal designed specifically for skilled workers, graduates, and professionals considering immigration to Germany. It provides plain-language guides on every visa type, step-by-step application processes, recognition of foreign qualifications, job search tools, and practical living information. This is the single most useful starting point for immigration applicants.
- **Key resources:**
  - Visa and residence permit guides (all types)
  - Qualification recognition wizard
  - Job board for international applicants
  - City guides and cost of living information
  - FAQ for specific countries and professions

---

### Federal Foreign Office — Auswärtiges Amt
- **URL:** https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/visa-service
- **Description:** The German Ministry of Foreign Affairs oversees all visa issuance processes through its network of embassies and consulates worldwide. This site provides country-specific visa requirements, required documents, appointment booking links, and processing times for every German embassy globally. All national visa (D-visa) applications for stays longer than 90 days are submitted here.
- **Key resources:**
  - Country-specific visa requirements and documentation lists
  - Links to local German embassy/consulate pages
  - Schengen short-stay visa (C-visa) information
  - National long-stay visa (D-visa) categories
  - Information for diplomatic and official passport holders

---

### Federal Employment Agency — Bundesagentur für Arbeit
- **URL:** https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/en
- **Description:** Germany's public employment agency manages the labor market, publishes the official shortage occupation list (Mangelberufsliste), and must grant approval (Zustimmung) for many work permit applications. For EU Blue Card and Skilled Worker visas, the BA conducts a labor market test to verify the job offer meets legal requirements. It also runs the official job portal for Germany.
- **Key resources:**
  - Official shortage occupation list (updated regularly)
  - Salary benchmarks by occupation and region
  - Labor market approval (Zustimmung) procedures
  - JOBBÖRSE — official German job search portal: https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/jobsuche
  - Guidance for employers hiring internationally

---

### Federal Ministry of the Interior — BMI
- **URL:** https://www.bmi.bund.de/EN
- **Description:** The Federal Ministry of the Interior drafts immigration legislation, including the Residence Act (Aufenthaltsgesetz) and the Skilled Immigration Act (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz). It sets the overarching legal framework within which BAMF and local immigration offices (Ausländerbehörden) operate.
- **Key resources:**
  - Full text of the German Residence Act
  - Skilled Immigration Act (2020 and 2023 amendments)
  - Policy papers on migration and integration
  - Citizenship and naturalization law

## 2. Visa Application & Entry

### Visa Appointment Booking — German Embassies Worldwide
- **URL:** https://service2.diplo.de/rktermin/extern/choose_realmList.do
- **Description:** The official online appointment booking system used by German embassies and consulates globally. Applicants select their country, the relevant embassy, and the visa category, then book a slot for their in-person interview. Wait times vary significantly by location — Tehran, Mumbai, and Beijing often have backlogs of several months.
- **Usage tip:** Book the appointment as early as possible, even before all documents are ready.

---

### German Missions Abroad — Embassy Finder
- **URL:** https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/about-us/auslandsvertretungen/deutsche-auslandsvertretungen-node
- **Description:** Complete directory of all German embassies, consulates-general, consulates, and honorary consulates worldwide. Each mission page lists its specific jurisdiction, accepted visa categories, required documents, fees, and local appointment system.

---

### Foreigners Registration Office — Ausländerbehörde
- **URL:** https://www.bamf.de/EN/Themen/MigrationAufenthalt/migrationaufenthalt-node.html
- **Description:** Once inside Germany, all residence permit matters are handled by the local Ausländerbehörde (foreigners office) of the city or district where the applicant lives. There is no single national URL — each city has its own office and online portal. Major city offices:
  - **Berlin:** https://www.berlin.de/einwanderung
  - **Munich:** https://www.muenchen.de/rathaus/auslaenderwesen
  - **Hamburg:** https://www.hamburg.de/einwanderung
  - **Frankfurt:** https://www.frankfurt.de/auslaenderbehoerde
  - **Stuttgart:** https://www.stuttgart.de/auslaenderbehoerde

## 3. Visa Types — Official Pages

### EU Blue Card
- **URL:** https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/eu-blue-card
- **BAMF page:** https://www.bamf.de/EN/Themen/MigrationAufenthalt/ZuwandererDrittstaaten/Migrathek/BlaueKarteEU/blauekarteeu-node.html
- **Description:** Official information on the EU Blue Card for highly qualified non-EU nationals with a university degree and a qualifying job offer. Covers salary thresholds, eligible occupations, application procedure, and path to permanent residence. The salary thresholds are updated annually by BAMF.

---

### Skilled Worker Visa (Degree)
- **URL:** https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/skilled-workers-with-academic-training
- **Description:** Official guide for university-degree holders seeking employment-based immigration under the Skilled Immigration Act (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz). Explains recognition requirements, job offer conditions, and the application process at German embassies.

---

### Skilled Worker Visa (Vocational Training)
- **URL:** https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/skilled-workers-with-vocational-training
- **Description:** Official guide for applicants with recognized vocational/trade qualifications (Berufsausbildung) seeking work in Germany. Covers the Recognition Partnership (Anerkennungspartnerschaft) option for those whose qualification is under review, allowing entry before full recognition is granted.

---

### Chancenkarte (Opportunity Card)
- **URL:** https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/chancenkarte-opportunity-card
- **Description:** Official page for the points-based job-seeker visa introduced in March 2024 under the Skilled Immigration Act reform. Explains the scoring criteria, financial requirements, permitted work during the stay, and conversion to a work visa upon finding employment.

---

### Job Seeker Visa
- **URL:** https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/job-seeker-visa
- **Description:** Official guide for the 6-month job-seeker visa for qualified professionals with a fully recognized foreign degree and at least 5 years of relevant work experience. Largely superseded by the Chancenkarte for most applicants.

---

### Ausbildung Visa (Vocational Training in Germany)
- **URL:** https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/vocational-training
- **Description:** Official information for non-EU nationals who have secured a vocational training contract (Ausbildungsvertrag) with a German company. Covers language requirements, financial provisions, application documents, and the path to long-term residence after completing the training.

---

### Student Visa
- **URL:** https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/student-visa
- **Description:** Official guide for the student visa (Visum zu Studienzwecken) required for non-EU nationals admitted to a German university. Covers the blocked account (Sperrkonto) requirement, health insurance, language proficiency requirements, and post-graduation job-seeker rights.

---

### Freelancer / Self-Employment Visa
- **URL:** https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/freelancers
- **Description:** Official information for freelancers (Freiberufler) and self-employed persons (Selbständige) seeking to work independently in Germany. Explains the distinction between regulated and non-regulated freelance professions, business plan requirements, and the discretionary assessment by local Ausländerbehörden.

---

### Family Reunification Visa
- **URL:** https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/family-reunion
- **Description:** Official guide covering all family reunification scenarios: spouse of German citizen, spouse of EU Blue Card holder, spouse of residence permit holder, and children joining parents. Includes the A1 language requirement exemptions for certain categories.

---

### Permanent Residence — Niederlassungserlaubnis
- **URL:** https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/living-permanently-in-germany
- **Description:** Official information on acquiring permanent residence in Germany. Covers all pathways (Blue Card, Skilled Worker, study, family), required years of residence, language requirements, and financial self-sufficiency conditions.

## 4. Degree & Qualification Recognition

### anabin — KMK Foreign Qualification Database
- **URL:** https://anabin.kmk.org/anabin.html
- **Operated by:** Standing Conference of the Ministers of Education and Cultural Affairs (KMK)
- **Description:** The authoritative database used by German authorities to assess the equivalency of foreign university degrees and higher education institutions. Each institution is rated H+ (recognized), H+/- (conditionally recognized), or H- (not recognized). Each degree type is also classified. This is the first database checked when applying for a Blue Card or Skilled Worker Visa.
- **Key tools:**
  - Institution search by country and university name
  - Degree type equivalency lookup
  - Country-level overview reports

---

### Anerkennung in Deutschland — Recognition Advisor
- **URL:** https://www.anerkennung-in-deutschland.de/en/interest/seeker
- **Operated by:** BIBB (Federal Institute for Vocational Education and Training)
- **Description:** An interactive step-by-step tool that guides applicants through the recognition process for foreign qualifications — both academic and vocational. Identifies the correct recognition body, required documents, expected timeline, and associated costs based on the applicant's specific qualification and target occupation in Germany.

---

### BIBB — Federal Institute for Vocational Education and Training
- **URL:** https://www.bibb.de/en
- **Description:** The federal authority responsible for vocational education and training in Germany. BIBB maintains the database of recognized vocational qualifications (Berufsabschlüsse) and coordinates the recognition process for foreign trade and vocational certificates. Essential for Skilled Worker Visa (Vocational) and Ausbildung Visa applicants.

---

### Anabin University & Institution Search
- **URL:** https://anabin.kmk.org/no_cache/filter/institutionen.html
- **Description:** Direct search interface within the anabin database to look up the status of any specific foreign university. Enter the institution name and country to see its official recognition status in Germany. Required check before submitting a Blue Card or Skilled Worker Visa application.

---

### Recognition of Medical and Healthcare Qualifications — IMM
- **URL:** https://www.imm.nrw.de (North Rhine-Westphalia example)
- **Description:** Medical, dental, nursing, and pharmacy qualifications are recognized at the state (Bundesland) level, not federally. Each state has its own authority. The IMM (Ingenieurkammer) and equivalent state bodies handle engineers and architects. Check your target state's specific recognition office.

## 5. Language & Integration

### Goethe-Institut — Official German Language Exams
- **URL:** https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf.html
- **Description:** The Goethe-Institut is the internationally recognized institution for German language certification. Its exams (Goethe-Zertifikat A1 through C2) are accepted by German embassies for family reunification visas (A1), Ausbildung visas (B1/B2), and integration purposes. Exams are offered at Goethe centers in most countries.
- **Exam levels:** A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2
- **Family reunification A1 exam:** https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf/prf/ga1.html

---

### TestDaF — German for University Admission
- **URL:** https://www.testdaf.de/en
- **Description:** TestDaF (Test Deutsch als Fremdsprache) is a standardized German language proficiency test specifically designed for university admission in Germany. It tests reading, listening, writing, and speaking at levels TDN 3–5 (equivalent to B2–C1). Most German-taught university programs require a minimum TDN 4 overall.

---

### DSH — Deutsche Sprachprüfung für den Hochschulzugang
- **URL:** https://www.dsh-germany.com
- **Description:** The DSH is a language proficiency exam administered directly by German universities. Unlike TestDaF, it is taken on-site in Germany (often after a preparatory language course). Passing DSH-2 is the standard requirement for admission to German-language university programs.

---

### BAMF Integration Courses
- **URL:** https://www.bamf.de/EN/Themen/Integration/ZugewanderteTeilnehmende/zugewanderteteilnehmende-node.html
- **Description:** Information on government-subsidized integration courses available to immigrants in Germany. Each course consists of a language component (600 hours, A1 to B1) and an orientation course (100 hours on German law, culture, and history). Completion of the integration course and passing the final test (DTZ) is required for certain residence permit extensions and naturalisation.

---

### telc — Language Tests for Visa and Residence
- **URL:** https://www.telc.net/en
- **Description:** telc GmbH offers standardized language tests recognized by German immigration authorities. The telc Deutsch A1: Zum Zuzug exam is specifically accepted for family reunification visa applications. Tests are available at certified centers worldwide.

## 6. Living & Working in Germany

### Germany Trade & Invest — GTAI
- **URL:** https://www.gtai.de/en
- **Description:** The federal agency for foreign trade and inward investment. GTAI provides detailed information for entrepreneurs, investors, and self-employed individuals on setting up a business in Germany, including legal structures, taxation, and sector-specific regulations.

---

### International Welcome Centers
- **URL:** https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/living-in-germany/welcome-culture/welcome-centers
- **Description:** A network of Welcome Centers (Willkommenszentren) located in cities and regions across Germany that provide free, multilingual advisory services to international skilled workers and their families. They assist with questions about recognition, housing, childcare, language courses, and administrative registration.

---

### Shortage Occupation List — Federal Employment Agency
- **URL:** https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/en/welcome-to-germany
- **Description:** The official and regularly updated list of professions experiencing labor shortages in Germany. Occupations on this list qualify for reduced salary thresholds under the EU Blue Card and faster processing for Skilled Worker Visa applications. Currently includes IT, engineering, healthcare, care work, and skilled trades.

---

### Public Health Insurance — GKV-Spitzenverband
- **URL:** https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/english/statutory_health_insurance.jsp
- **Description:** Overview of Germany's statutory (public) health insurance system. All employees earning below the insurance threshold (Versicherungspflichtgrenze, ~€69,300/year in 2024) are automatically enrolled in public health insurance. International applicants can also voluntarily enroll. Major public insurers include TK, AOK, Barmer, and DAK.

---

### Blocked Account Providers (Sperrkonto) — for Student Visa
The German embassy requires proof of a blocked account for student visa applicants. Official or widely accepted providers:

| Provider | URL |
|---|---|
| Deutsche Bank | https://www.deutsche-bank.de/pk/konto-und-karte/konten/sperrkonto.html |
| Fintiba | https://www.fintiba.com |
| Expatrio | https://www.expatrio.com |
| Coracle | https://www.coracle.de |

> **Note:** Fintiba and Expatrio are the most commonly used by international students and are officially accepted at all German embassies.

---

### DAAD — German Academic Exchange Service
- **URL:** https://www.daad.de/en
- **Description:** The DAAD is the world's largest funding organization for the international exchange of students and researchers. It provides scholarships for international students coming to Germany, publishes the most comprehensive database of German university programs (including tuition fees, language of instruction, and application deadlines), and offers grants for research stays.
- **University program search:** https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/studienangebote/international-programmes/en

---

### uni-assist — University Application Portal
- **URL:** https://www.uni-assist.de/en
- **Description:** The central application service for international students applying to German universities. Most German universities use uni-assist to process applications from non-EU/EEA nationals. Applicants submit documents once through uni-assist, which verifies and forwards them to the chosen universities.

## 7. Legal Texts & Regulations

### German Residence Act — Aufenthaltsgesetz (AufenthG)
- **URL:** https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/index.html
- **English summary:** https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_aufenthg/index.html
- **Description:** The primary law governing the entry, residence, and employment of foreign nationals in Germany. All visa categories, residence permit types, rights of holders, and conditions for permanent residence and citizenship are defined here. The 2020 and 2023 Skilled Immigration Act amendments (Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz) are integrated into this act.

---

### Skilled Immigration Act — Fachkräfteeinwanderungsgesetz
- **URL:** https://www.bmi.bund.de/EN/topics/migration/skilled-workers-immigration-act/skilled-workers-immigration-act.html
- **Description:** The 2019 act (with major 2023 reform effective March 2024) that significantly expanded Germany's immigration pathways for skilled workers. It introduced the Chancenkarte (points-based system), extended the Blue Card to more occupations, created the Recognition Partnership pathway, and eliminated the priority check (Vorrangprüfung) for most skilled worker applications.

---

### EU Blue Card Directive
- **URL:** https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32021L1883
- **Description:** The revised EU Blue Card Directive (2021/1883/EU), in force since November 2023, which updated the EU-wide framework for highly qualified third-country workers. Germany's national implementation introduced lower salary thresholds and broader eligibility.
